Bus stop thermal comfort assessment

Collected field data at transit stops across the Denver metropolitan area to study how heat affects people waiting for the bus. Recorded environmental and meteorological measurements with Kestrel monitoring equipment, and documented the infrastructure, vegetation, and site characteristics that influence pedestrian comfort.

The work supports transportation planning and urban heat mitigation strategies.
